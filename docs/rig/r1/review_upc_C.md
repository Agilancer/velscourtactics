## Systemic (across many figures)

1. **Floor contact (a retarget problem, not a single clip).** In the Walk almost no figure gets a foot back to its rest height in any of the six frames. The lowest foot is 2-14 cm up, Maudlin 12-21 cm, and the hips ride up to 10-21 cm above rest. The Swing drives the back foot 7-11 cm through the floor on paladin_m and slime_lord_m. Several attacks hold the whole body off the floor (ninja_m Slash +4 cm, ranger_m Shoot +5 cm) or in it (necromancer_m Bolt -4 cm). The root or hip height does not seem to be rescaled to each body's leg length. Fix that before bm's walk and jog go in, or they will float the same way.
2. **Upper arms are short.** On 13 of 15 figures the shoulder-to-elbow length is 0.10-0.12 of the skull height, against the reference's 0.133-0.153, while the forearms are in range. The shoulder joint is being pushed out to the pauldron's edge (paladin_m) or down the humerus (skeleton). Fit the shoulder inside the ball of the shoulder, not to the clothing outline.
3. **Shoulder depth.** In the side view the fitter puts shoulder joints at the back edge of the arm: ninja_m and slime_lord_f by about 4 cm, stevie by about 3 cm.
4. **Knee offset ignores the leg.** The knee's distance in front of the hip-ankle line ranges from -1.6 cm (slime_lord_m) through 0 (ninja_m, ninja_f) and 1 cm (mortimer, ranger_f) to +8.5 and +10.5 cm (the necromancers, whose knees sit on the robe's front surface; necromancer_f's also bow 6 cm outward). Target 2-2.5% of stature measured on the leg, not on the garment.
5. **Neck and head chain off the neck.** On ninja_f the head pivot is near the face. On slime_lord_f and ranger_f the neck joint sits back in the collar or nape. On stevie it sits at the gorget front. The spine bodies are mostly straight, but the last two links are not.
6. **Left-right mismatches a mirror check would catch.** Shoulders differ by 3-5 cm on necromancer_m, slime_lord_m and ranger_f; Maudlin's ankles and knees differ by about 2 cm; one knee sits lower on ninja_f and mortimer's R elbow sits low. Mirror the fitted joints across the spine and flag any difference over 1 cm.
7. **Gait (one line):** the Mixamo walk bounces and kicks the shin up behind, so these figures read springy and weightless next to bm's planted hero walk. Combined with item 1 this is the biggest problem at the game's camera, bigger than any single joint.
8. **Cloth (out of scope, worst cases):** paladin_m's tabard and paladin_f's gown in the Run, Maudlin's apron in the Walk, ranger_m's feather train.
