# Rig review r2, group C: critic 2 (up-and-comer)

How I measured: every rig and motion card, then the numbers in `r2/rest` and `r2/motion` (joint heights from the
floor mark, side-view depth, knee offset from the hip-ankle line, left-right mirror), with the same numbers run on
`r1/rest` to see what moved. I zoomed the rest and motion renders with the skeleton redrawn over them and the floor
line drawn in. "Lowest foot" is the lowest ankle or ball joint relative to its own rest height. Card motion frames are
numbered 1-6 from left to right (frame 6 is frame 1 again: the clips loop). Stature fractions are only quoted where
they mean something: every figure in this group wears a hat, hood, crown, helmet or crow, so the card % runs low.

The headline: the floor pass, the spine pass and the mirror pass all landed. Eleven of thirteen figures now stand,
walk and attack with a foot on the floor (lowest foot 0.0-1.9 cm in every Walk frame, -1.1 to +2.7 cm in the attacks),
every spine is straight up the middle, and every left-right pair is mirrored. bm's walk reads right on all of them:
heel strike with the toes up, a planted stance, a clean toe-off. What is left is two figures the passes missed
(Lisandra's clips were never grounded, necromancer_m's knees went the wrong way) and the known toe spikes on the skeleton.

## Systemic, ranked by how many figures they hit

1. **Upper arm shorter than the forearm (12 of 13 figures; all but the skeleton). Minor on each, but it is everywhere.** Shoulder to elbow measures 15.7-20.2 cm against forearms of 16.3-22.5 cm (0.092-0.110 of the model height), where bm's heroes have the upper arm as long as or longer than the forearm (0.133-0.153 against 0.127-0.137). Where the elbow is visible (bracers, couters) it is right, so the shoulder joint is still being placed high and out in the clothing (paladin_m, necromancer_f). On the skeleton, where the bone shows, the fitter got 25.7 cm. Fit the shoulder at the centre of the ball of the shoulder (about 4-5 cm below the top of the deltoid) and check the ratio before shipping.
2. **The Run reads as a bound, not a jog (13 of 13; gait, not the rig).** At the contact frame (Run frame 4) the hips drop 17-22 cm below rest. In frames 3 and 5 both feet are 18-26 cm off the floor. In frame 1 the trailing shin kicks up level with the knee, ankle 34-40 cm up. That is an 11% bob on a 1.8 m figure. From the iso camera it will look like hopping, and on the gowned figures (lily, lisandra, paladin_f) it tears the cloth worst. The walk, by contrast, matches bm's reference well. Halve the vertical in the jog.
3. **The right foot rides high in the attacks (11 of 13; known, not handled).** The R knee is bent 5.5-9 cm forward in frame 1 against the L's 1.5-2 cm, and the R ankle or ball sits 1.2-2.7 cm above rest through the Bolt, Shoot, Slash and Swing on every figure except maudlin (0.6 cm) and lisandra (who hovers whole). Small at the camera; minor.
4. **Knees under the target (6 figures: necromancer_f 0.65-1.0%, ranger_f, mortimer, skeleton and paladin_m 1.1-1.2%).** These are under the refit's own 1.2% floor and short of bm's 2-2.5%. They bend the right way in every strip, so this is minor, but the floor rule is not being enforced on all of them.
5. **The knee fit landing on the garment (necromancer_m; a regression and a blocker).** The knee went from 10.5 to 18.1 cm forward, onto the robe's front corner, while the same body on necromancer_f came out right. Add a check that the knee lies within a few cm of the hip-ankle line and inside the leg's own silhouette below the hem.
6. **A figure the floor pass missed (lisandra; a blocker).** Her Bolt still hovers 12.7 cm and her Walk 6 cm; everyone else in the group is on the floor to within 2 cm. Her clips need the same set-down.
7. **Toe spikes on the bare-footed dead (skeleton; known; a blocker).** See the skeleton block. It is the only bare-footed figure in this group, and it fails on that alone.
8. **Cloth through the floor (out of scope, worst cases):** ranger_m's feather train 34 cm (Walk), lisandra's gown 24 cm and paladin_f's gown 23 cm (Walk), maudlin's cloak 13 cm and paladin_m's cape or tabard 14 cm while standing (Bolt, Swing). The cloth pass needs floor collision, not just leg collision.
